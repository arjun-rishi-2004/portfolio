01 / Production systemsRoaming

Connecting charging networks

Different operators need to exchange trusted, consistent data before a driver can roam between networks. I own OCPI onboarding and integration flows, including credentials and synchronization of locations, tariffs, tokens, and sessions.

Scope: integrations with 5+ charge point operators and 15+ mobility service providers.

02 / Product engineeringAccess

Starting a charge without an app

A charging session should not depend on installing an app first. I’ve helped design backend authentication, authorization, and transaction flows that let drivers begin a session at the charger.

Focus: less friction for drivers and a dependable service-side session flow.

03 / PrototypeEnergy

Making energy exchange interoperable

I built foundational marketplace workflows for participant discovery, price negotiation, and transaction settlement using the Beckn protocol and India’s energy stack.

Focus: a practical path from protocol design to a working peer-to-peer flow.
